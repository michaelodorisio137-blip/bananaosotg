# AzeezOS — development continuity ledger

## Permanent project direction (2026-11-27, US Eastern)
The RG DS is the reference platform for a personal AI-assisted custom game environment, not merely a frontend. Make successful changes transferable to future devices: isolate portable renderer algorithms, emulator adapters, UI, device-specific display/input support, and build/recovery infrastructure. Preserve upstream pins, source changes, exact working binaries and release hashes, tests, hardware observations, and unsuccessful experiments. Never equate compilation, a published OTA, and actual device success. Do not overwrite original ROMs or discard previous working firmware. No claim of universal full-speed Wii compatibility: this is an aspirational research target, not a verified capability.

## Display contract
For native integer mode, center the original raster within a logical 320x240 canvas and scan out exactly 2x to 640x480 on RG DS. DS original 256x192: native origin (32,24), physical origin (64,48); GBA original 240x160: native origin (40,40), physical origin (80,80). Preserve native pixel mapping and touch alignment. Extra area must be newly rendered rather than stretched; unsupported exterior coverage is a known limitation. Separate 2D pixel presentation from selective 3D texture filtering and HD geometry. Native and HD variants must preserve the same camera center. Do not sacrifice existing speed or working features without documenting the tradeoff.
